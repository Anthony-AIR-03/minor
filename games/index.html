Game skill tree

Eight games, one route: from recognising your first HTML tag to building a small website on your own. Every game trains one level of Bloom's taxonomy, and you unlock the next one by passing the game before it.

Playable Up next Locked

How unlocking works

  1. Play the open game.

    Each game has three levels and ends with a score.

  2. Pass it with at least 80%.

    Only your first try per task counts. Passing shows you master the skill; extra points don't buy your way in.

  3. The next game opens.

    After Build the DOM the tree splits: Style Lab and Layout Builder open together, and Bug Hunter waits until you have passed both.

The Bloom ladder

Higher in the tree doesn't mean more questions; it means a different kind of thinking. Each step asks something new of you.
